The short version

The App does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-ins, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. Information you save in the App (like favorites) stays on your device and never reaches us or anyone else.

Information we collect

None. The App does not collect any personal data, usage data, or device identifiers. We have no servers; the App has nothing to send data to.

Information stored on your device only

The App lets you save preferences — for example, marking menu items as favorites or remembering your last order so you can reorder faster. This information is stored only in the App's private storage on your device. It never leaves your device, is not visible to us or to any third party, and is deleted when you delete the App. You can also clear it any time by removing and reinstalling the App.

When you contact or order from CRUDE

Actions like tapping "call," "email," "get directions," or sending an order request open your device's own phone, mail, or maps application. Anything you then send (such as an order email with your name and phone number) goes directly to CRUDE through your own email service, exactly like any email you write — the App itself does not see, store, or transmit it. Information CRUDE receives that way is handled like any other customer correspondence and used only to fulfill your request.

Third-party services

The App contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. Links that open other services (Instagram, Apple Maps) take you to those services, which have their own privacy policies.

Data retention, deletion, and your choices

Because we collect nothing, there is nothing for us to retain, delete, correct, or export, and no consent to revoke. On-device preferences are under your control as described above. If you believe the App has handled information in a way this policy doesn't describe, contact us at hello@crudecoffeebar.com and we will investigate.

Children

The App is a general-audience app for viewing CRUDE's menu, hours, and location. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the App changes what information is handled (for example, adding optional push notifications), we will update this policy before that version is released and update the "effective date" above. Material changes will be noted in the App's store listing.
